The commands listed below are documented in the reference manual
but currently don't have a remote version.  There are a bunch of
reasons why that might be: they deal with internal structures,
they use keyword params and too much code uses them to adjust
that (often a similar replacement is available), not possible
for macros that evaluate a body of code (like no-output), it's
obscure or rarely used and just hasn't been added, depends on
an underlying mechanism which itself hasn't been updated, etc.

Many of them could be added, and likely will over time, but
others may end up being dropped from the 'supported' interface
or remaining 'internal only'.
